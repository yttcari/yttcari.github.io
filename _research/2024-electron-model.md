- Integrated physically motivated electron temperature models into the General Relativistic Ray Tracing (GRRT) code `ipole` to simulate accretion flows.
- Evaluated model predictions against Event Horizon Telescope (EHT) Sgr A\* constraints, focusing on polarimetric signatures and temporal variability.
