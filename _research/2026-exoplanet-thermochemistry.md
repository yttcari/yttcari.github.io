- Built a Python thermochemistry toolkit that computes heat capacity, enthalpy and entropy from NASA-9 polynomial coefficients, which feeds reaction energetics into the Kompot chemical network.
- Built a statistical mechanics pipeline to estimate thermochemistry for exotic species missing from NASA-9, such as ArH<sup>+</sup>, HeH<sup>+</sup>, H<sub>3</sub><sup>+</sup> and N<sub>2</sub>H<sup>+</sup>.
