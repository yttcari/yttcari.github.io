title: "White Dwarfs as Detectors of Sub-Asteroid Mass Primordial Black Holes"
collection: research
permalink: /research/wd-pbh
date: 2026-09-01
period: "Ongoing"
---

- Implemented an iterative shooting method to achieve self-consistent profiles for white dwarfs containing a central Primordial Black Hole (PBH).
- Demonstrated the possible survival of critical-mass PBHs due to accretion and quantum effects.
- Characterized the thermal signatures of asteroid-mass PBHs, establishing new observational constraints on PBH abundance from observed white dwarf surface temperatures.
